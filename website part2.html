Pizza

Pizza is a globally popular dish consisting of a flat base of leavened wheat dough. It is typically topped with a savory tomato sauce, melted cheese (most commonly mozzarella), and various other ingredients, then baked at high temperatures.

Originating centuries ago in Naples, Italy, as a humble street food for the working class, pizza has evolved into countless regional styles. Whether you enjoy the thin, chewy crust of a traditional Neapolitan pizza or the thick, comforting wedges of a Chicago deep-dish, it remains one of the world's most versatile and beloved foods

What is Biryani

Biryani is an aromatic, celebratory mixed rice dish from the Indian subcontinent. It combines long-grain Basmati rice, slow-cooked marinated meat (like chicken, lamb, or goat), and a complex blend of spices. The components are layered and cooked together in a pot, often sealed, allowing the rich flavors to deeply infuse the rice.

Quality Ingredients

Biryani is a complex, aromatic Indian dish built on four essential pillars: long-grain Basmati rice, marinated meat or vegetables, whole and ground spices, and caramelized onions. These elements are layered together and slowly cooked (or dum steamed) so the flavors meld into a rich, cohesive feast.
